How we work

Four steps. Zero surprises.

You'll always know what's happening, when it's happening and what it costs.

STEP 01

Free site visit and quote

Chris or Bart comes out, has a proper look, talks through finishes and options, then sends a clear written quote. No pressure, no hidden extras.

STEP 02

Prep, formwork and steel

We dig out, box up, lay the membrane and tie the steel. If you need earthworks, stormwater or other trades, we line them up for you.

STEP 03

Pour day

Trucks booked, crew on site early, concrete placed, screeded and finished by hand. You get updates as we go, so you're never left guessing.

STEP 04

Clean up and handover

We strip the formwork, tidy the site and walk you through care and curing. All that's left is the concrete and a tidy yard.

Good questions

How much does a concrete driveway cost in Brisbane?

It depends on the size, access, how much dig out is needed and the finish you choose. Plain concrete sits at the lower end and exposed aggregate or coloured finishes cost more. We'll come out, measure up and give you a fixed written quote for free.

How soon can I drive on my new driveway?

As a rule of thumb you can walk on it after a day or two and park a car on it after about a week. We'll give you exact advice for your job at handover.

Do you do exposed aggregate and coloured concrete?

Yes. Decorative work is a big part of what we do, including exposed aggregate, coloured concrete, Spraycrete and Covercrete resurfacing. We'll walk you through the options.

Can you organise the other trades?

Yes. We regularly coordinate earthworks, stormwater and other trades so you've got one point of contact from start to finish.

Do you take on small jobs?

Too right. A side path gets the same care as a full commercial slab.
